---
title: New look, with light and dark themes
version: 0.1.0
merged: 2026-10-13
kind: Improved
---
GitLanes has a new orange look on warm neutrals, designed for light and dark alike.

- Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark** in View → Theme or the ⌘K menu, or press ⌃⌘T to cycle. Menus, the title bar and dialogs follow the theme.
- Text and graph lanes meet WCAG AA contrast in both themes, and the lane colours stay distinct with common kinds of colour blindness.
- Warnings now use amber and destructive actions use red, so neither is confused with the accent colour.
